The idea

We don't network.
We connect.

The most valuable opportunities rarely begin with a transaction. They begin with a person. A conversation. A shared interest. A problem someone else can solve. An introduction neither person knew they needed.

Connect The Spots brings exceptional people together across entrepreneurship, investment, sport, wellness, entertainment, philanthropy and culture. Not to build the biggest network. To build the right relationships.

Good business. Meaningful good. That's the double bottom line.

The connector

Meet Daniel Jeffery.

Founder, Connect The Spots
Entrepreneur
Community Builder
Mental Health Advocate

Daniel Jeffery is an entrepreneur, connector and community builder who believes the most meaningful opportunities in life and business begin with genuine human connection.

His mission extends beyond business. Daniel is passionate about mental health advocacy and breaking the stigma around vulnerability, particularly among entrepreneurs, leaders, athletes and men who often feel pressure to carry everything themselves. He believes some of the most important conversations happen when we're willing to take off the armor, acknowledge we don't have all the answers, and create space for people to be honest about what they're going through.

His own journey has reinforced a simple belief: strength isn't always about having it together. Sometimes strength is having the courage to ask for help, be vulnerable, and choose growth.

That philosophy is woven into everything he builds. Through curated experiences (private dinners, golf, wellness, travel, philanthropy and intimate conversation), Daniel creates environments where people connect beyond titles and transactions. Because behind every founder is a person. Behind every athlete is a human being. Behind every leader is a story. And sometimes the right conversation, at the right moment, changes everything.

The common thread is connection.

The process

One conversation can create the next five.

  1. Discover

    Understand the person, opportunity and objective.

  2. Connect

    Identify the people, capital, resources or relationships that matter.

  3. Facilitate

    Create the right introduction, room or conversation.

  4. Activate

    Move beyond the introduction toward something tangible.

  5. Grow

    Create economic value, meaningful impact, or both.
